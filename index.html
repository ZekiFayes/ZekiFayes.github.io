A Unified Continuous Framework for Flow Matching
Posted on August, 31, 2026

Contemporary flow-matching methodologies frequently treat the training probability distribution and the discrete inference schedule as decoupled hyperparameter engineering tasks. This study presents a strictly unified continuous-time mathematical framework, formally proving that stochastic probability sampling during training and deterministic schedule warping during inference are mathematically dual operations. We establish a Universal Generative Theorem demonstrating that the continuous inference velocity must be scaled precisely by the inverse of the training probability density to satisfy absolute numerical stability requirements.

Flow Matching for Thermal-Driven Wood Design
Posted on July, 2026

In computational materials science, the ultimate objective of material engineering is the reverse: discovering a structure that yields expected physical properties, i.e., inverse material design. In the context of bio-inspired materials like wood, current research has explored propositional design processes on two-dimensional surfaces with mono-modal thermal properties and has gained more foundational knowledge of wood thermal behavior. To advance this design process further, we tackle this inverse wood design challenge by reversing the conventional structure-property-behavior pipeline, i.e., thermal-driven wood design, which translates wood thermal behavior to its structure.
